Safety Disclaimer

PlainShelter · EqualInformation, LLC

PlainShelter provides general information drawn from public-domain and open sources. It is not professional or emergency-service advice.

Survival situations are dangerous and conditions vary. Follow local emergency guidance, and call your local emergency number when you can. Check structures, snow, trees and ground for hazards before and while you build. Never run a fuel burner or fire in a sealed shelter: ventilate, and watch for carbon monoxide.
